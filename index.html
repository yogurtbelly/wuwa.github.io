Wuthering Waves

An unofficial fan page for the open-world action RPG by Kuro Games.

About the game

Wuthering Waves is a free-to-play action RPG released in 2024. You play as the Rover, who wakes up in a world damaged by a disaster called the Lament. Combat is fast, with dodging, parrying and switching between three characters.

Beginner FAQ

Echoes are spirits of defeated enemies. You equip them to a Resonator for stats and skills.

Waveplate is a resource spent on boss fights and challenges for rewards. It refills over time.

A Convene is a pull on a banner, the game's gacha system for characters and weapons.
